Junior Frontend Engineer
Northwind Payments — Lagos (hybrid, 3 days in office)

About the role
We are a payments company serving small merchants across West Africa. You will join a team of
six engineers working on the merchant dashboard: the screens our customers use every day to see
what they have been paid, settle disputes and download statements.

What you will do
- Build and maintain screens in our React and TypeScript dashboard
- Work with our designers to turn Figma files into accessible, responsive pages
- Write tests for the code you ship and review your teammates' pull requests
- Talk to merchants about what is confusing, and fix it

What we are looking for
- Comfortable with JavaScript and React, and willing to learn TypeScript properly
- Understands REST APIs and can debug a request that is failing
- Uses Git day to day
- Writes tests without being asked
- Communicates clearly in writing

Nice to have
- Next.js
- Experience with Kubernetes and Go
- Kafka or another event streaming platform
- AWS, particularly ECS and CloudWatch
- Experience at scale: systems serving millions of users

How to apply
Send your CV. We interview in two rounds: a technical conversation and a short take-home.
